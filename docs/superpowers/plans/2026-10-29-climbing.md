# Climbing Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:**
- Bars, boxes and a tilting switch as field elements.
- A stock `climber` part whose hooks hold the robot by contact.
- Climb signals with per-range scoring.
- A `scrimmage climb run` script runner, and a scenario that climbs the 2022 HANGAR to HIGH.

**Architecture:**
- Elements extend `scrimmage/testrange/elements.py` and `build_elements.py`. Bars and non-ground boxes go in a
  non-ground geom group.
- The climber is a stock part in `scrimmage/parts/stock/climber.py`: telescope slides, J hooks of capsules, an
  optional passive latch hinge with a `release` slot, and an optional pivot.
- Climb signals live in `scrimmage/terrain/climb.py`, run beside the TerrainMonitor.
- The script runner is `scrimmage/climb/` (a run module and the CLI). It drives joints through `World.external`, like
  the terrain sweep's driver.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11+, MuJoCo ≥ 3.4, pytest.

**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-29-climbing-design.md

## Global Constraints

- **2022 HANGAR** (research notes, from manual §5.4.2 and GE-22100):
  - rung tops at 48.72, 60.26, 75.59 and 90.94 in; the upper three are measured from floor protection 0.375 in high;
  - MID→HIGH→TRAVERSAL steps of 24.00 in horizontal and 15.34 in up;
  - pipe 1.66 in OD;
  - truss 12 in square; structure 9 ft 0.25 in wide, 10 ft 0.75 in deep, 6 ft 2 in tall;
  - levels LOW, MID, HIGH, TRAVERSAL score 4, 6, 10, 15.
  - Every number is tagged `# source:` or `# estimate:`.
- **2020 switch:** ±14.5° hinge, about 42 kg, CoM 0.66 m below the shaft, level within 8°.
- **2013 pyramid:** rungs at 30, 60 and 90 in; 1.5 in tube.
- **Hook friction presets (estimates):** steel 0.5, polycarbonate 0.35, UHMW 0.15, nitrile 1.0.
- **Rung compliance in 2022-hangar:** 160 kN/m (estimate), with light damping.
- **Contacts:** hook contacts use condim 3 with the pipe's real radius. A 60 kg hang sinks under 2 mm.
- **Scoring rules:** `[climb] rule` is "lowest-rung" (2022), "hang" (2020, with `level_bonus`) or "lowest-point"
  (2013, with no sequential check).
- **2022 robot limits:** G107 at most 16 in beyond the frame perimeter; G106 at most 66 in tall in the HANGAR ZONE.
- **Existing stock mechanisms stay collide = false.** Existing ranges and robots are unchanged and lockstep stays
  bit-identical.
- **License:** never transform com.ctre classes, interpose CTRE natives, decompile Phoenix or use non-public C APIs.
- **Commits:** end every message with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.
- **CI:** never push. Pin floats with tolerances, and keep each new test well under 20 s.

## Review Focus

1. A robot under a bar on the floor: its wheel rays and ground gap ignore the bar (not ground), and it isn't hanging.
   (Task 1 test.)
2. A robot hanging by one hook from two bars at once, or touching the truss: the level follows the rule (the lowest
   rung touched), and the truss doesn't void it. (Task 4 test.)
3. A climber whose telescope tube sweeps into a rung: the tube collides and the climb run reports "hit a bar".
   (Task 2 and Task 5 tests.)
4. A latch with no `release` motor: the hook can still release by geometry. A latch held closed under load doesn't
   leak. (Task 3 test.)
5. A climb script step waiting on a condition that never comes (a missed hook): it times out with a clear failure
   row, not a hang. (Task 5 test.)

### Task 2: The climber part

- Modify: `scrimmage/parts/model.py` if a hook material preset key is needed

**Interfaces:**
- **Stock `climber` params:** stages, travel, base length, motor, count, gearing, spool radius (as the elevator),
  hook throat (default bar OD + 6 mm), hook depth, hook material (steel, polycarbonate, uhmw or nitrile), hook count
  (1 or 2 across the arm), latch (none, passive or released), latch spring, pivot (none or articulated), pivot range,
  pivot gearing and mass.
- **Slots:** `extend` (motor) and `extend_encoder`; `release` (motor, when latch = released); `pivot` and

  - all collide;
  - hooks are tagged so signals can find them, with names `<inst>.hook<i>.<piece>`;
  - hook geoms use condim 3, with friction from the material;
  - tubes and hooks use ROBOT_BITS.
