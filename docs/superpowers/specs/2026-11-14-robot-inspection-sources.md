# FRC robot size and weight limits, 2013 to 2026

All numbers below were read by me (pdftotext) in the primary-source PDFs listed. "Read: yes" means I read the quoted text in the primary PDF in this session. Read 2026-11-14 by a research agent for the robot inspection design.

Conventions: "frame perimeter" is the sum of the side lengths of the polygon from a string wrapped round the frame in the bumper zone (2013 to 2024 "FRAME PERIMETER", 2025 and 2026 "ROBOT PERIMETER"). Minor protrusions of 1/4 in. or less (bolt heads, fastener ends, weld beads, rivets) are not part of it (2013-2025 R-rule on the perimeter; 2026 "0.25in"). No season has a minimum perimeter in the rules I read (searched for "minimum"/"at least" near the perimeter rules; none found).

## 2013 (Ultimate Ascent)

Read: yes, all items.

1. Weight. R05 (section 4.1.1.5): "The ROBOT weight may not exceed 120 lbs." Excluded: (A) robot battery and its half of the Anderson quick connect pair (no more than 12 in. of cable per leg, lugs, bolts, insulation); (B) BUMPERS (including covers). Bumper weight: R23 "Each set of BUMPERS (including any fasteners and/or structures that attach them to the ROBOT) must weigh no more than 20 lbs." (R23 number taken from the manual's table of contents, section 4.1.6.2.)
2. Frame perimeter. R03-A: "total length of the FRAME PERIMETER sides may not exceed 112 in." Definition R02: perimeter contained in the BUMPER ZONE, fixed non-articulated structure, minor protrusions of 1/4 in. or less excluded. Bumper zone (R25) is 2 in. to 10 in. above the floor. No minimum.
3. Starting configuration height. No separate start height. R03-C: "height may never exceed 84 in. tall."
4. Extension. No "beyond the perimeter" limit. Instead a cylinder: G23 "While not in contact with the PYRAMID, a ROBOT'S horizontal dimensions may never exceed a 54 in. diameter vertical cylinder." G23-1 (added 2013-01-15): while in contact with the pyramid the robot may not exceed a 54 in. cylinder and may not extend beyond a vertical plane offset 54 in. from the pyramid base. In-match height, G22: "If in contact with the carpet in its AUTO ZONE and/or its PYRAMID, <= 84 in. Otherwise, <= 60 in." (the "<=" symbol is garbled in the text layer; the Team Update says the same).
5. Other. R03-B: playing-configuration horizontal dimensions never exceed a 54 in. diameter vertical cylinder.
Mid-season changes: TU 2013-01-15 re-issued G23, G23-1, R03 (the 54 in. cylinder wording and the 84 in. height are as quoted); TU 2013-01-11 re-issued R05 (120 lbs unchanged).

Starting configuration: no overhang (read 2026-11-14 in the manual, pdftotext). R04 (section 4.1.1.4): "In the STARTING CONFIGURATION, no part of the ROBOT may extend outside the vertical projection of the FRAME PERIMETER, with the exception of minor protrusions such as bolt heads, fastener ends, rivets, etc." (no bumper exception in the 2013 text)

## 2016 (Stronghold)

Read: yes (manual). Team update PDF text partly garbled; no change to the limits below found.

1. Weight. R5: "The ROBOT weight must not exceed 120 lbs." Excluded: ROBOT BUMPERS and the battery with its half of the Anderson pair (no more than 12 in. of cable per leg, lugs, bolts, insulation). Bumper weight R20: "Each set of BUMPERS ... must weigh no more than 20 lbs."
2. Frame perimeter. R3-A: "total length of the FRAME PERIMETER sides must not exceed 120 in." R2: perimeter in the BUMPER ZONE, minor protrusions of 1/4 in. or less excluded. Bumper zone R22: between 4 in. and 12 in. above the floor. No minimum.
3. Starting height. R3-C: "ROBOT STARTING CONFIGURATION height must not exceed 54 in. (note that ROBOT height may exceed this limit in-MATCH as allowed by G17)." In-match height G17: "may not exceed 4 ft. 6 in. during the MATCH, except during the final twenty (20) seconds of TELEOP where there is no height limit when a ROBOT is fully contained by the opponent's COURTYARD." (54 in. = 4 ft. 6 in.)
4. Extension. G18 and R3-B: "ROBOTS may not extend more than 15 in. beyond their FRAME PERIMETER." No one-side-only rule.
5. R4: in starting configuration nothing outside the vertical projection of the frame perimeter except bumpers and minor protrusions.

Starting configuration: no overhang (read 2026-11-14 in the manual, pdftotext). R4: "In the STARTING CONFIGURATION (the physical configuration in which a ROBOT starts a MATCH), no part of the ROBOT shall extend outside the vertical projection of the FRAME PERIMETER, with the exception of its BUMPERS and minor protrusions such as bolt heads, fastener ends, rivets, etc."

## 2020 (Infinite Recharge)

Read: yes. Unchanged by Team Updates (R5 re-issued in the March 2020 TU with the same 125 lbs).

1. Weight. R5: "The ROBOT weight must not exceed 125 lbs. (~56 kg)." Excluded: bumpers; battery and its half of the Anderson pair (no more than 12 in. of cable per leg, lugs, bolts, insulation); location-detection tags if provided by the event. Bumper weight R23: "Each set of BUMPERS (including any fasteners and/or structures that attach them to the ROBOT) must weigh no more than 15 lbs. (~6 kg)." Bumper zone R18: floor to 7 1/2 in.
2. Frame perimeter. R3: "may not have a FRAME PERIMETER greater than 120 in. (~304 cm)". R1 as above. No minimum.
3. Starting height. R3: "may not be more than 45 in. (~114 cm) tall." In-match height G17: "may not exceed 45 in. (~114 cm) above the carpet during the MATCH, with the exception of ROBOTS intersecting their ALLIANCE'S RENDEZVOUS POINT during the ENDGAME." Measured as if on a flat floor.
4. Extension. R4 and G18: "ROBOTS may not extend more than 12 in. (~30 cm) beyond their FRAME PERIMETER." No one-side rule.
5. R2 no overhang in starting configuration.

Starting configuration: no overhang (read 2026-11-14 in the manual, pdftotext). R2: "In the STARTING CONFIGURATION (the physical configuration in which a ROBOT starts a MATCH), no part of the ROBOT shall extend outside the vertical projection of the FRAME PERIMETER, with the exception of its BUMPERS and minor protrusions such as bolt heads, fastener ends, rivets, cable ties, etc."

## 2022 (Rapid React)

Read: yes. No Team Update changed these numbers (TU only changed the G107 figure).

1. Weight. R103: "must not exceed 125 lbs. (~56 kg)". Excluded: bumpers; battery plus its half of the Anderson pair (no more than 12 in. of cable per leg, lugs, bolts, insulation); event location tags. Bumper weight R407: "Each set of BUMPERS ... no more than 15 lbs. (~6 kg)." No combined robot-plus-bumper limit rule found. Bumper zone R402: floor to 7 1/2 in.
2. Frame perimeter. R104: "FRAME PERIMETER greater than 120 in. (~304 cm)". No minimum.
3. Starting height. R104: "may not be more than 4 ft. 4 in. (~132 cm) tall." In-match G106: height "may not exceed the maximum STARTING CONFIGURATION height (4 ft. 4 in.) unless any part of the ROBOT'S BUMPERS is in its HANGAR ZONE, in which case its height may not exceed 5 ft. 6 in. (~167 cm)." Also G109: never beyond the PURPLE PLANE (disabled).
4. Extension. R105 and G107: "ROBOTS may not extend more than 16 in. (~40 cm) beyond their FRAME PERIMETER." No one-side rule.
5. G108: no bumper higher than the HIGH RUNG for more than a moment.

Starting configuration: no overhang (read 2026-11-14 in the manual, pdftotext). R102: "STARTING CONFIGURATION – no overhang. In the STARTING CONFIGURATION (the physical configuration in which a ROBOT starts a MATCH), no part of the ROBOT shall extend outside the vertical projection of the FRAME PERIMETER, with the exception of its BUMPERS and minor protrusions such as bolt heads, fastener ends, rivets, cable ties, etc."

## 2023 (Charged Up)

Read: yes. Numbers unchanged by TUs (TUs 01, 06, 11 only edited G107/G108/G109 text and figures).

1. Weight. R103: "must not exceed 125 lbs. (~56 kg)". Excluded: bumpers; battery plus its Anderson half (no more than 12 in. cable per leg, lugs, bolts, insulation); event tags. Bumper weight R407: "no more than 15 lbs. (~6 kg)". No combined limit rule. Bumper zone R402: floor to 7 1/2 in.
2. Frame perimeter. R104: "greater than 120 in. (~304 cm)". No minimum.
3. Starting height. R104: "may not be more than 4 ft. 6 in. (~137 cm) tall." In-match G106: "may not exceed 6 ft. 6 in. (~198 cm) above the carpet during the MATCH" (flat-floor measurement).
4. Extension. R105: "may not extend more than 48 in. (~121 cm) beyond their FRAME PERIMETER." G107: "may not extend beyond their FRAME PERIMETER in more than 48 in. (~122 cm). MOMENTARY and inconsequential extensions ... are an exception." G109: may not extend beyond the perimeter in more than one direction (over 1 side) at a time, and the extension may not reach outside the projection of that side; exceptions for momentary extensions, a robot fully inside its LOADING ZONE or COMMUNITY, and momentary movement between adjacent sides. G108: a robot with bumpers intersecting the opponent's LOADING ZONE or COMMUNITY may not extend beyond its frame perimeter at all.

Starting configuration: no overhang (read 2026-11-14 in the manual, pdftotext). R102: "STARTING CONFIGURATION – no overhang. In the STARTING CONFIGURATION (the physical configuration in which a ROBOT starts a MATCH), no part of the ROBOT shall extend outside the vertical projection of the FRAME PERIMETER, with the exception of its BUMPERS and minor protrusions such as bolt heads, fastener ends, rivets, cable ties, etc."

## 2024 (Crescendo)

Read: yes.

1. Weight. R103: "must not exceed 125.5 lbs (~56 kg)". Team Update 21 (April 9, 2024) changed it from 125 lbs to 125.5 lbs (radio change for the VH-109). Excluded: bumpers; battery plus its Anderson half (no more than 12 in. cable per leg, lugs, bolts, insulation); event tags. Bumper weight R407: "Each set of BUMPERS ... no more than 15 lbs. (~6 kg)". No combined limit rule. Bumper zone R402: floor to 7 1/2 in.
2. Frame perimeter. R104: "greater than 120 in. (~304 cm)". No minimum.
3. Starting height. R104: "may not be more than 4 ft. (~121 cm) tall." In-match height G413-A: "its height, as measured when it's resting normally on a flat floor, may not exceed 4 ft. (~122 cm)". (TU01 corrected the cm conversions only.)
4. Extension. R105 and G413-B: "may not extend more than 12 in. (~30 cm) beyond their FRAME PERIMETER." (G413-B in TU01 changed the cm figure from 40 to 30 to match 12 in.) No one-side rule. Exception for damage not used for strategic benefit.
5. R106: no mechanism that reduces the working length of the stage chain.

Starting configuration: no overhang (read 2026-11-14 in the manual, pdftotext). R102: "STARTING CONFIGURATION – no overhang. In the STARTING CONFIGURATION (the physical configuration in which a ROBOT starts a MATCH), no part of the ROBOT shall extend outside the vertical projection of the FRAME PERIMETER, with the exception of its BUMPERS and minor protrusions such as bolt heads, fastener ends, rivets, cable ties, etc."

## 2025 (Reefscape)

Read: yes (manual V11 plus combined TUs). Rule text now says ROBOT PERIMETER.

1. Weight. R103: "must not exceed 115 lbs. (~52 kg)". TU00 (Jan 4, 2025) states the weight was "reduced from 125 lbs. to 115 lbs." (note 2024 was 125.5). Excluded: bumpers; battery plus its Anderson half (no more than 12 in. cable per leg, lugs, bolts, insulation); event tags. Combined weight: R408 "The total weight of the ROBOT (as described in R103) with BUMPERS must not exceed 135 lbs. (~61 kg)". There is no separate bumper-set weight rule in 2025 (the old 15 lb rule is gone; 135 - 115 = 20 lb of bumpers implied, my arithmetic). Bumper zone R405: padding must fill 2 1/2 in. to 5 3/4 in. above the floor; R403 bumpers extend no more than 4 in. from the perimeter.
2. Perimeter. R104: "ROBOT PERIMETER greater than 120 in. (~304 cm)". No minimum.
3. Starting height. R104: "may not be more than 3 ft. 6 in. (~106 cm) tall." I found no in-match height limit rule for 2025 in the G rules (searched "height", "tall", "exceed"). So the 3 ft. 6 in. is a starting-configuration limit only; gap noted: the field-specific section 7.4.3 refers to "height and extension restrictions for various areas of the FIELD" but I found no numeric height rule there.
4. Extension. R105 and G415: "may not extend more than 1 ft. 6 in. (~457 mm) beyond the vertical projection of their ROBOT PERIMETER" (G415 says ~45 cm). No one-side rule in 2025.

Starting configuration: no overhang (read 2026-11-14 in the manual, pdftotext). R102: "STARTING CONFIGURATION – no overhang. In the STARTING CONFIGURATION (the physical configuration in which a ROBOT starts a MATCH), no part of the ROBOT shall extend outside the vertical projection of the ROBOT PERIMETER, with the exception of its BUMPERS and minor protrusions such as bolt heads, fastener ends, rivets, cable ties, etc."

## 2026 (Rebuilt)

Read: yes (manual is Version TU22, which is the final TU of the season; the combined TU PDF was also searched).

1. Weight. R103: "must not exceed 115.0lb (52.16kg)". Excluded: (A) bumpers; (B) battery and its Anderson half (including wire, lugs, connecting bolts, insulation; the 12 in. cable cap is no longer in the text); (C) event location tags. Combined: R408 "The total weight of the ROBOT (as described in R103) with BUMPERS must not exceed 135.0lb (61.23kg)". No bumper-only weight rule found. Bumper zone R405: padding fills the zone 2.5 in. to 5.75 in. from the floor; R403 bumpers extend no more than 4.0 in.
2. Perimeter. R104: "may not have a ROBOT PERIMETER greater than 110.0in (2.794m)". Down from 120 in. in 2025. No minimum.
3. Starting height. R104: "may not be more than 30in (76.2cm) tall." In-match R107 (enforced by G413): "ROBOTS may not extend such that their total height exceeds 30.0in (76.2cm)", measured as if on a flat floor. So the same 30 in. applies throughout the match (TRENCH clearance is 22.25 in. under the arms).
4. Extension. R105: "may not extend more than 12in (30.48cm) beyond the vertical projection of their ROBOT PERIMETER." R106: horizontal extension in only one direction (over 1 side) at a time, and not outside the projection of that side; a round section counts as infinitely many sides; exceptions for bumpers, minor protrusions, and momentary extensions (TU edits added the momentary and flex exceptions, G413-C and G413-D, and a 1.5 in. flex allowance). R108: extensions may not lift the bumpers out of the bumper zone by interacting with carpet, bumps or tower base.
5. G413 violation is a MAJOR FOUL.

Starting configuration: no overhang (read 2026-11-14 in the manual, pdftotext). R102: "STARTING CONFIGURATION – no overhang. In the STARTING CONFIGURATION (the physical configuration in which a ROBOT starts a MATCH), no part of the ROBOT shall extend outside the vertical projection of the ROBOT PERIMETER, with the exception of its BUMPERS and minor protrusions such as bolt heads, fastener ends, rivets, cable ties, etc."

## Battery weight (12 V 18 Ah SLA)

Vendor and manufacturer values do not agree to the pound. The FRC rule bounds are in the game manual.

- FRC rule (primary, read): R601-E (2026, 2025, 2024, 2023, 2022 manuals; R32 in 2020; R31 in 2016): "Nominal weight: 11.0lb to 14.5lb (4.99kg to 6.57kg)" (older manuals: "11lbs. to 14.5 lbs. (~5 kg. to 6.5 kg.)"). 2026 text: https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2026/Manual/2026GameManual.pdf
- AndyMark am-0844 (MK ES17-12), vendor listing, read: "Weight: 12.89 lbs. each battery" with "Size: 7.13 in. long x 6.57 in. tall x 2.99 in. wide". The listing also says the double-box shipping assembly of two batteries weighs about 29 pounds (shipping, not battery). URL: https://www.andymark.com/products/mk-es17-12-12v-sla-battery-set-of-2 (page said "Sold out" when I fetched it).
- MK Battery ES17-12 datasheet (manufacturer, as hosted by REV Robotics; the PDF text layer is overprinted and contains two versions): "Weight Approx. 12.89Lbs. (5.86kg)" and "Weight Approx. 6.0kg(13.2Lbs.)". URL: https://revrobotics.com/content/docs/ES17-12_User_Guide.pdf. The two figures disagree inside the same PDF, so MK's own weight is 12.89 to 13.2 lb depending on datasheet revision.
- EnerSys Genesis NP18-12B (manufacturer range summary, read): 13.6 lb / 6.17 kg, 7.13 x 3.00 x 6.57 in. URL: https://www.enersys.com/4a1436/globalassets/documents/product-documentation/genesis/np/amer/amer-en-rs-np_0625.pdf
- Not found from a primary source: a weight from a manufacturer page for the ES17-12 other than the two above; third-party retailer figures (about 10.9 lb for an AJC equivalent, an MK datasheet copy at baterias.com giving 13.82 lb in a web search snippet) are secondary and marked unverified.

Practical value: about 13 lb (12.89 lb AndyMark, 13.2 lb MK, 13.6 lb EnerSys). It is excluded from robot weight in every season above, so it only matters for total mass in a simulator (add it to the 115 or 125 lb figure for a legal-robot total, plus bumpers).
